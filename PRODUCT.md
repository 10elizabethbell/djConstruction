# D&J Construction — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (GitHub Pages), Cal Sans from Google Fonts, no frameworks, no tracking
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page site for a local renovation contractor)

## Users
- Homeowners in and around Leamington, Ontario planning an interior renovation, most often a bathroom. They arrive from Facebook (group posts, the page) on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner(s) of D&J Construction, who receive the site as a gift and judge it first.

## Purpose
Give D&J Construction a real front door: who they are, what they renovate, where, and a two-tap way to ask for a quote (text or call). Today their only presence is a Facebook page and a promo flyer.

## Positioning
A Leamington renovation crew that specializes in bathrooms and takes on all interior work. Their own words: "We specialize in Bathroom renovations but do all interior renovations." Slogans on their flyer: "Quality renovations built to last." / "Your vision. Our expertise." / "Let's build something great together."

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone 519-818-8755 (from their flyer); whether it takes texts is unknown. Email Djconstruction@mail.com (flyer) — footer only.
- Facebook page: https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100086518921872 (760 followers, seen 2026-10-21).
- No published prices, hours, reviews, owner name, years in business, or work photos (the page says photos are coming).
- Do not merge with Dancore Additions and Renovations (a different Leamington business).

## Brand commitments
- Logo: round badge, white on charcoal slate (#2e373c), crossed hammers, "RENOVATIONS · D&J · CONSTRUCTION".
- Flyer adds a warm tan/sand accent (sampled ~#cdac8d) on near-black.
- Voice: plain and friendly, "we" (they post as a crew: "We are new to this group").

## Principles
1. Phone first: the first phone screen shows who, what, where and the way to ask.
2. Only their facts. No invented prices, reviews, years, guarantees or "free" offers.
3. Bathrooms lead; everything else is "and all interior work".
4. Speak as the crew ("we"), about how they treat a customer's home.
